"""QA-A-252g step 1 (leader decision on QA-A-252f, P2): the exposure variation between the frames of a dose group is a component common to all pixels, not the temporal noise of a pixel,
so it is taken out BEFORE the group variance is formed. Method: every frame of a group is scaled to the mean dose of the group (the frame's mean net value against the group mean of
those), x_k = f_k * (p_k - D) with f_k = group mean signal / signal_k; the variance about the group mean is formed on the x_k (row and column means of every frame out, as before). The local
reference is formed from that variance image, so it is on the same footing. The line persistence (temporal median of the group, per-frame residuals) uses the same scaled values.
Reason for scaling instead of differencing the frames: the group mean and the median of the persistence stay defined for groups of three and more, and the same scaling is the one the
quantum gain estimator already uses."""

        s = s.replace(old, new)
