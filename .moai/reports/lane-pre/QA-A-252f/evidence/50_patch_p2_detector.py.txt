After gain correction the measured temporal variance of a pixel in the bright frames of one dose, against the local expectation, is a defect from 2 times (3.01 dB) on, and it has to
persist over the dose groups. The gain is the MEASURED response of the pixel (the relative response of the offset-corrected frames against its local median), not 1 / r of a model.
The frame-count bias of the sample variance (P5) was corrected before (the local reference is the median of the variances divided by the median of chi-square / nu); this patch
adds the gain correction, the persistence over the dose groups and the same gain correction for the line stretches (their statistic and their per-frame persistence)."""
base = 'D:/workspace-github/xpe-pre/modules/preprocess/'


def edit(rel, pairs):
    s = open(base + rel, encoding='utf-8', newline='').read()
    crlf = '\r\n' in s
    s = s.replace('\r\n', '\n')
    for old, new in pairs:
        assert s.count(old) == 1, (rel, s.count(old), old[:100])
        s = s.replace(old, new)
    open(base + rel, 'w', encoding='utf-8', newline='').write(s.replace('\n', '\r\n') if crlf else s)
