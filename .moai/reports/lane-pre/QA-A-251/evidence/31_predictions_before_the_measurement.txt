QA-A-251 predictions for the first-generation defect map, written after the tool, its tests and the mutation checks and before the measurement ran.
Reasoning basis: the first-generation generator (xpe_bpm_generate, defaults) flags a pixel from (a) the mean of the dark frames against the local median of a 32 x 32 window with
a threshold of 8 x MAD, and (b) the mean of the bright frames against the mean of a 128 x 128 window with +-7 %. It looks at no temporal noise and no per-dose response.
Confidence is stated; none of these is a result.

 R1 Pixel defects, detection rate (share of the 64 planted pixels flagged):
    dead a=1.0 and a=0.5: at least 0.95 (the bright rule sees a 50-100 % loss). slope g=0.75 and 1.25: at least 0.95; g=0.9 and 1.1: between 0.5 and 1.0 (10 % against a 7 % band).
    hot h=200, 100, 50: at least 0.9; h=25: between 0.2 and 0.8 (the dark threshold is about 8 x the local spread of the dark mean, which I estimate at 20-30 pixel values).
    nonlinear c=0.4: at least 0.9; c=0.2: between 0.4 and 1.0. Confidence about 70 %.
 R2 noisy pixels (m=4, 2, 1): 0.0 to 0.15 at every level. The first-generation generator uses the mean of the dark frames only, so added temporal noise (reduced by the square
    root of 7 in the mean) stays under the threshold. This is the gap the second generation must close. Confidence about 80 %.
 R3 Blocks (dead, 2 x 2 to 8 x 8, a=1.0 and 0.5): detection rate at least 0.9 for every size (every pixel of the block deviates by 50-100 % from the 128 x 128 mean).
    Confidence about 75 %.
 R4 Lines: dead and nonlinear lines at least 0.9 for the full, partial and pair shapes at a=1.0 (and at least 0.5 at a=0.5 and c=0.2); noisy lines 0.0-0.15.
    The one-pixel-wide full row or column does not disturb the 128 x 128 window mean enough to hide itself. Confidence about 65 %.
 R5 Control bands, false detections out of 12: fixed_band h=100 flagged in both directions (dark mean shifted by 100 against a threshold near 25), h=30 flagged in at least one direction,
    gain_band g=0.8 and 1.2 flagged in both directions (20 % off the window mean), varying_band s=20 and 8 not flagged (the 7-frame mean leaves about 7.6 and 3.0).
    So I expect between 6 and 10 of 12 control bands called a false detection. Confidence about 60 %.
 R6 New false alarms (flagged only in the planted run, outside the truth widened by 2): between 0 and 40. Planted pixels sit at least 8 pixels from each other and
    the window statistics change little. Confidence about 60 %.
 R7 Split-half Jaccard (all pixels): between 0.5 and 0.9, higher inside the truth (deterministic dead and slope pixels appear in both halves) than outside it (natural defects
    near the threshold appear in one half only). Confidence about 55 %.
 R8 Correction error |planted - clean| / local sigma, defect correction on: median between 0.3 and 2.5 for the kinds that are flagged (an interpolated pixel differs from the
    clean pixel by about one local sigma); for undetected kinds (noisy) the median is the injected size over the local sigma. Without defect correction the median for dead
    pixels is above 20. Confidence about 60 %.
 R9 D_n: dead and nonlinear full lines exceed 3 predicted sigmas without defect correction (huge ratios); with the first-generation correction applied most are within 3 sigma.
    Noisy lines stay within 3 sigma everywhere (the line mean of zero-mean noise does not move). Confidence about 50 %.
 R10 Natural defects: the clean map for Dark_08..14 (without Dark_07) is near the 134 pixels of the earlier map (between 100 and 250). Confidence about 70 %.
 R11 Time: each of the two full set generations about 6-8 minutes; each half map about 3-5 minutes.
